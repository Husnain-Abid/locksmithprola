Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Emergency, Automotive, Residential, and Commercial locksmith services.

Do you provide 24/7 emergency locksmith service?
Yes, our emergency locksmiths are available 24/7 for lockouts, urgent repairs, and break-ins. Call us anytime for immediate assistance.
Can you unlock modern vehicles with electronic locks?
Yes, we can unlock most vehicles including those with smart keys, transponder chips, and keyless entry systems.
What residential services do you offer?
We offer home lockout services, lock installation, rekeying, smart lock upgrades, and security consultations for homeowners.
I lost my house keys. Can you help?
Absolutely. We can rekey or replace your locks quickly to secure your home and provide new keys.
Can you upgrade my old locks to smart ones?
Yes, we can upgrade your existing locks to keyless, biometric, or smart locking systems for added security and convenience.
What commercial locksmith services do you offer?
We offer access control, master key systems, panic bar installation, lock repairs, and full-scale security audits for businesses.
Can you install keypad or card access systems?
Yes, we install and maintain access control systems including keypad, card reader, and biometric entry systems for commercial properties.
Do you offer maintenance contracts for businesses?
Yes, we provide flexible locksmith service agreements that cover routine maintenance, upgrades, and emergency support for business clients.
How quickly can you respond to a commercial lockout?
Our average response time is 20–30 minutes within most service areas. We prioritize commercial lockouts to minimize business disruption.
Can you install high-security locks?
Yes, we install high-security and restricted keyway lock systems for both residential and commercial applications to prevent unauthorized access.
